For app questions or a problem with your budget, contact info@eb-net.org. Include your app and iOS versions. Do not send bank passwords or authentication codes.

Common questions

How does safe-to-spend work?

Payday Loom checks your available account balances, upcoming bills, savings reservation, and minimum buffer through the next payday. Expected income stays excluded until you mark it received. It uses the lowest projected balance and checks each account's ability to cover its own payments.

My balance already includes a transaction. Should I add it again?

No. Record only transactions not yet reflected in your entered balance. Mark a scheduled bill paid from Timeline so it is reconciled once. Update an account balance in Settings when reconciling it with your bank.

Can I connect Postbank?

Live bank connections are not available in the current release candidate. Postbank is the first planned bank adapter. Enter available balances and transactions manually for now.

Why is iCloud not syncing?

Check the iCloud status in Payday Loom Settings, your internet connection, iCloud storage, and Apple Account sign-in. Keep the app open and tap Sync with iCloud now. If two devices have changes, export a copy before choosing which budget to retain. Do not delete the app while it has unsynced changes.

Why is Apple Intelligence unavailable?

The assistant requires a compatible device, supported availability, Apple Intelligence enabled, and an installed system model. The rest of Payday Loom works without the assistant. Use the calculation breakdown for the same financial facts.

How do I export or delete my budget?

Use Settings → Prepare data export, then the share sheet. Delete all budget data clears this device and queues a cleared revision for iCloud. Check that synchronization succeeds so the deletion reaches other devices. Previously exported files and device backups require separate management.

Are estimates guaranteed?

No. Missing bills, outdated balances, uncertain timing, and changed plans can affect the result. Keep your entries current and confirm important payments with your financial institution.
