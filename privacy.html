Your budget stays yours.

PayCycle has no application backend, advertising network, tracking SDK, or analytics service. We do not receive your budget records or assistant conversations.

What the app stores

PayCycle stores the balances, income schedules, bills, recorded transactions, savings allocations, scenarios, and preferences that you enter. A local SwiftData copy supports offline use. When iCloud is configured and available, budget records synchronize as an encrypted revision in your private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account.

Sensitive budget content uses CloudKit encrypted fields. Apple operates iCloud; its storage, encryption, account recovery, and key availability follow your Apple Account settings and Apple's terms. This policy does not promise that every iCloud account configuration provides the same end-to-end encryption guarantees. Device backups may include the app's local data according to your iOS backup settings.

The app displays sync failures and pending changes. Concurrent edits on multiple devices may require you to select which complete budget revision to retain.

On-device Apple Intelligence

On compatible devices, Apple's on-device Foundation Models model interprets questions. PayCycle does not use Private Cloud Compute or an external AI provider. Only your current question, bounded recent user questions, and the previous action are passed to the on-device model; numerical answers are rendered from PayCycle's deterministic calculations. Questions and responses are not saved by PayCycle or synchronized to iCloud. Apple controls model availability and system-managed model downloads.

Optional app lock

You can protect access using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework verifies your identity; PayCycle receives only the result, never face scans or fingerprints. The preference stays on this device. When enabled, a privacy screen covers the app as it becomes inactive and it locks when backgrounded. Authentication is required to enable or disable protection.

Assistant messages remain only in memory while the conversation is open and are cleared when closed or backgrounded.

Network connections and permissions

  • CloudKit communicates with Apple's iCloud services to check your account and synchronize encrypted budget revisions.
  • Notifications are optional and scheduled locally. Notification previews do not include account names, amounts, or transaction titles.
  • Opening support, privacy, or email links launches the relevant browser or mail app. Those services follow their own privacy policies.
  • The current version does not connect to banks, initiate payments, or send financial information to a banking aggregator.

PayCycle does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone. There are no advertising identifiers or tracking permissions.

Your controls and retention

Your records remain until you edit or delete them. Settings lets you export your records as JSON through the system share sheet. Exports contain financial information and are controlled by the destination you choose.

Delete all budget data clears this device's financial records. The cleared budget revision replaces the iCloud copy after a successful synchronization. Offline changes or unresolved conflicts remain pending until synchronization completes. Other devices receive that cleared revision on their next successful sync. A small empty revision and synchronization metadata remain to prevent deleted financial data being silently restored by another device.

Previously exported files, old device backups, and copies you have shared are outside PayCycle's deletion control. Manage iCloud storage and iOS backups in your Apple Account settings. We cannot restore your private iCloud records or recover lost encryption keys.

Website and contact

This support website uses no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or cookies. Its hosting provider may process standard connection data such as IP addresses to serve and secure the website. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Avoid sending bank passwords, authentication codes, or complete financial exports.
